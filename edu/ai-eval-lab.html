1. The task & answer to grade

A retail store's revenue fell in June. An AI gave this answer:

Revenue fell in June because the store ran out
of stock of the best-selling item. Moving
forward, the store should order more inventory.

Grade it on each criterion (tap PASS/FAIL).

Score: 0%

2. The rubric & why

Evaluation is atomic & binary so it's reproducible. Six criteria, weighted:

  • Correctness (30) — is the conclusion right?
  • Completeness (15) — all parts answered?
  • Reasoning (20) — is each claim supported by a step?
  • Source citation (15) — tied to a data source?
  • Risk & edge cases (10) — uncertainty noted?
  • Clarity (10) — readable & structured?

This answer asserts a cause with no evidence, so Correctness, Reasoning, and Citation fail. Note a correctness fail is mandatory — an incorrect answer fails regardless of how tidy it is. That's the same rubric design used by real AI evaluation teams.
